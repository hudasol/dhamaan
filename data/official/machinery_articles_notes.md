## Findings
1. Entry into force: 19 July 2023 (EUR-Lex metadata; consolidated Art. 54 "twentieth day following" publication; corrigendum Art. 47(2) 13->19 July 2023). Not seen in the authentic Art. 54 sentence.
2. Application: 20 January 2027. The corrigendum (OJ L 169, 4.7.2023) changed Art. 54 second paragraph from 14 January 2027 to 20 January 2027 (and Arts 51(2), 52(1)). So the EP Observatory page (14.1.2027) reflects the uncorrected original print. Commission and EU-OSHA say 20 January 2027.
3. Art. 3(16), 10(1)-(3), 18, 20(1), 21, 25(2)-(4): rows' paraphrases match the extraction. Gaps: A06 omits Art. 18(3); A14 (Art. 6(7)) "serious inherent risk" and examples not seen; A15/D43 recital paraphrase goes beyond the fragments returned.
4. Recitals 54 and 55: only the two fragments quoted in the items file.
5. Reg 2026/1744: dated 8 July 2026; page header 24.7.2026; in force 27 July 2026 (metadata; Recital 46: third day after publication). Changes to 2023/1230 seen only in the consolidated text: Art. 8 new third and fourth paragraphs (delegated acts under Art. 47, apply by 2 August 2028), Art. 20(10) (presumption via AI Act Arts 40 and 41 until standards exist), Art. 47(2) (delegation five years from 27 July 2026). Operative Arts 2 and 3 not read.

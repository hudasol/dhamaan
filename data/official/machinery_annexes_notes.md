# Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 annexes: official source check (2026-10-12)

## What could and could not be read
- WebFetch of the EUR-Lex HTML (CELEX:32023R1230) returned an extraction that ends partway through Art. 37 (168,475 characters). The PDF view ends around Art. 35, and the OJ:L:2023:165:FULL view around Art. 28 to 37. The ELI URL returned metadata only. No Annex I, III or IV text was returned by any official URL. I tried each canonical variant once and did not use mirrors.
- All EUR-Lex content below is a tool extraction with short verbatim snippets, not raw text. I therefore record `summary seen`, not `full text seen`, for everything.
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (CELEX:32026R1744) text was returned up to AI Act Art. 75d (about 135,000 of 149,656 characters); the final operative articles, including whatever amends 2023/1230, were not returned.

## Findings
1. Annex I Part A ML entry: item number and wording NOT verified (not accessible). Official cross checks only: Art. 6(1) sends Part A to Art. 25(2); Art. 25(2) lists module B+C, H or G; Art. 25(3) lets Part B use module A if applicable harmonised standards or common specifications were applied. Recitals 54 and 55 are consistent with the ML entry. Table claims of "categories 5 and 6" (B36) are unverified.
2. Annex III 1.1.9 exists: Art. 20(9) cites 1.1.9 and 1.2.1 for protection against corruption and safety and reliability of control systems (cybersecurity certification presumption). 1.1.9 text not read.
3. Retention periods. No article (10, 11, 13) requires keeping safety decision data. The table's Annex III attributions come from MAAK (law firm). The only other lead is an unofficial reproduction (ai-resources.eu, summary via WebFetch, not an official source): one year retention of safety decision data in 1.2.1 second list point (b); five year tracing log of interventions and software versions in 1.2.1 point f; 1.1.9 reported without a period. This conflicts with A17, which puts the five year log in 1.1.9. Status: open, must be checked in the Official Journal Annex III 1.2.1.
4. Annex III section 3 (autonomous mobile machinery, supervisory functions): not accessible; item numbers in A19 unverified.
5. Annex IV: only articles seen. Art. 10(2) points to Annex IV Part A; Art. 10(3) 10 year retention and source code on reasoned request (confirms A20 note); Art. 14(7) points importers to Part B. Annex IV headings not read.
6. Reg (EU) 2026/1744: recital 42 (delegated acts amending Annex III of 2023/1230, to apply by 2 August 2028) and recital 45 seen; ELI metadata: document date 2026-07-08, first entry into force 2026-07-27. Operative text, any change to Annex III wording, and the 5 year delegation are NOT verified.

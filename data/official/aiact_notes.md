# EU AI Act official source check (retrieved 2026-10-12)

Tools: an automated web fetch step and web search only. The fetch step returns a machine made extraction of the page, not raw text, so nothing here is better than "summary seen" apart from document header fields. The EUR-Lex text of 2026/1744 is cut off by the tool at about 150,000 characters, inside the amendment to Art. 75d (Article 1 point 32). The original 2024/1689 page is cut off inside recital 104, so no article of the original Act was reachable. AI Act Service Desk, data.europa.eu and publications.europa.eu fetches were refused for lack of permission (no answer) and were not retried.

## Confirmed
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 is dated 8 July 2026 (EUR-Lex title, metadata, header).
- OJ L series header date 24.7.2026 (EUR-Lex HTML). The study's 24 July is the publication date; 8 July is the adoption date; 27 July is entry into force. These are three events, not conflicting dates.
- Entry into force 27 July 2026: EUR-Lex metadata, Commission news page, and Recital (46) (third day after publication). The operative article itself was not read, so status is open in the items file.
- New high-risk dates 2 Dec 2027 (Art. 6(2), Annex III) and 2 Aug 2028 (Art. 6(1), Annex I): Recital (40) of 2026/1744 and Commission pages. The Art. 113 amending point and paragraph numbers were not reached. Do not cite an Art. 113 paragraph.
- Art. 2(2) replaced (point 2(a)): Section B products get only Art. 6(1), Art. 60a and Arts 102 to 112. B16 paraphrase supported. New Art. 2(13) exists and is not in the table.
- Art. 6 new paras 1a, 1b, 1c match B14.
- Art. 10: paras 1 and 6 replaced, para 5 deleted. Art. 11(1) second subparagraph SME simplification. Art. 17(2) proportionality. Art. 72(3): guidance and template by 2 Sep 2027.
- Draft high-risk classification guidelines (published 19 May 2026, updated 23 July 2026) and EN 18286 approved "in June" (CEN-CENELEC).

## Conflicts and corrections
- B32: official text adds a new subparagraph to Art. 40(2), not a new paragraph 3. The explorer was wrong, Orrick right.
- B28: Art. 43(3) was replaced (notified body designation deadline 28 Jan 2028); the paraphrase follows older text.
- B33: Modulos "12 July" for EN 18286 approval is not supported; the CEN-CENELEC page says June.
- B21, B24, D40: "full text seen" rests on the unofficial site only; proposed downgrade to summary seen.
- Not in the table but relevant: Art. 42(3) new (cybersecurity presumption for Reg 2024/2847 products), Art. 60 now covers Annex I Section A systems, new Art. 60a for Section B.

## Machinery reading (not settled)
Art. 2(2) as amended supports the study's description of what applies to Section B products. Whether machinery sits in Section B (item 21) could not be checked, because the Annex I amendment and the amendments to 2023/1230 (B19: Arts 8, 20, 47) lie after the cut-off. The reading stays "per explorer, unconfirmed". Under Art. 6(1a) to 1c a non-safety AI function in a robot is not a safety component.

# Short reading list

All items were retrieved during this study (11 and 12 October 2026). Row IDs refer to `data/evidence_table.csv`. Everything listed is a free page or file. Standards are represented by their free catalogue pages only; none is needed to use this repository.

1. **AMLAS (University of York)** (B44): AMLAS: the most concrete staged method for assuring machine learning components (open, free).  
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01564
2. **SACE (University of York)** (B46): SACE: system level safety case process for autonomous systems, with GSN patterns (open, free).  
   https://www.york.ac.uk/media/assuring-autonomy/documents/guidancedocuments/SACE_v1_1.pdf
3. **GSN Community Standard (SCSC-141C)** (D01): GSN Community Standard: the notation behind the worked example diagram.  
   https://scsc.uk/scsc-141c
4. **Safety Case Templates for Autonomous Systems (Adelard, for Dstl)** (B47): Safety case templates for autonomous systems: reusable argument patterns.  
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.02625
5. **Defeaters and Eliminative Argumentation in Assurance 2.0** (D09): Defeaters and eliminative argumentation: how to stress test a safety case.  
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15800v1
6. **EASA MLEAP final report (Machine Learning Application Approval)** (D21): EASA MLEAP report: learning assurance concerns (data completeness, generalisation, robustness).  
   https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/mleap-d4-public-report-executive_summary_expanded-issue01.pdf
7. **JARUS SORA Main Body Ed. 2.5 (JAR-DEL-SRM-SORA-MB-2.5)** (C15): JARUS SORA 2.5 main body: the drone risk and assurance method (read the Safety Portfolio step first).  
   https://www.caa.cz/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/SORA-v2.5-Main-Body-Release-JAR_doc_25.pdf
8. **EASA AMC & GM to Reg (EU) 2019/947 (Annex to ED Decision 2025/018/R)** (C34): EASA AMC annex for drone operators (in the second edition the table row is title and scope only, so read the annex yourself before relying on its GNSS text).  
   https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/annex_to_ed_decision_2025-018-r_1_2.pdf
9. **GCAA CAR-UAC (UA Commercial and Governmental Operations)** (C51): GCAA CAR-UAC Issue 02: UAE commercial and governmental drone operations (title and scope only in the second edition; download it and read the limits yourself).  
   https://www.gcaa.gov.ae/en/epublication/EPublications/Civil%20Aviation%20Regulations%20(CARs)/EMERGING%20TECHNOLOGIES/UNMANNED%20AIR%20SYSTEMS%20(UAS)/CAR-UAC%20-%20UNMANNED%20AIRCRAFT%20(UA)%20COMMERCIAL%20AND%20GOVERNMENTAL%20OPERATIONS%20-%20ISSUE%2002.pdf
10. **Cabinet Resolution No. (110) of 2023 on the UAE scheme for drone products and systems** (C48): UAE Cabinet Resolution 110/2023: drone product conformity and test list.  
   https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2177/download
11. **Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 (Machinery Regulation)** (A04): EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230: Article 10 manufacturer obligations (note application from 2027).  
   https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32023R1230
12. **UL 4600** (B07): UL 4600 Edition 3 update (news item): what the safety case must show; the standard itself is paywalled.  
   https://www.ul.com/news/ul-4600-edition-3-updates-incorporate-autonomous-trucking
13. **BSI PAS 1883 ODD taxonomy for an ADS** (D11): BSI PAS 1883: free ODD taxonomy including dust, sand and illumination attributes.  
   https://www.bsigroup.com/globalassets/localfiles/en-gb/cav/pas1883.pdf
14. **NASA-STD-7009 (Standard for Models and Simulations), version B** (D26): NASA-STD-7009: credibility assessment for models and simulations.  
   https://standards.nasa.gov/node/263
15. **EASA Safety Information Bulletin 2022-02 R4 (GNSS outage and alterations)** (D52): EASA SIB 2022-02R4: GNSS jamming and spoofing operational guidance.  
   https://ad.easa.europa.eu/blob/EASA_SIB_2022_02R4.pdf/SIB_2022-02R4_1
16. **ISO/IEC TR 5469** (B38): ISO/IEC TR 5469 catalogue page: AI in functional safety (paywalled).  
   https://www.iso.org/standard/81283.html

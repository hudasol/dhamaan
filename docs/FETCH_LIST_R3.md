## C. Real GNSS data, by hand

1. Open the Yunnan University GNSS interference data set on the Mendeley data service and find Part III (jamming and spoofing, 21 December 2023). The Part I record lists no files and states no licence, so record the licence from the page before any use.
2. Open the page of the Jammertest 2024 vehicular paper and find whether any data are released and under which licence. The page could not be retrieved by the automated service.
3. Open the data page of the Finnish Geospatial Institute jammer test and read the licence statement. The data are raw samples, so a software receiver would be needed to produce position fixes.
4. Read the terms of service of the PX4 flight log service. Logs are kept for 12 months, so download any log needed before it is deleted.
5. Record every chosen data set, its licence text and its URL in data/third_party_manifest.csv before any replay.

# Pilot partner brief (one page)

**Status: proposal.** A student proposal seed, not a deployment, certification or offer of service. No body has reviewed, requested or endorsed it.

## What the work is

A source traced evidence base on what robot and drone safety instruments ask a developer to show, a checkable assurance case, a test protocol for Gulf conditions (heat, dust, glare, GNSS degradation), and preregistered simulations. The simulations are synthetic; they show how the checks behave, not how a real robot behaves.

## What a pilot would add

Bench and field evidence from a robot the partner already owns: logged runs in a fixed format, scored by the same checks, entered into an assurance case with their hashes. The checker keeps a claim about hardware open until such evidence exists.

## What a pilot would need

- A robot or sensor head that can log position, odometry, range sensor health, camera saturation and component temperatures.
- A test area that can be closed during runs, and a named safety observer.
- A person who can confirm the operating domain and the component limits.

## What is available to a partner

- The log specification, schemas, bench sheets and stop list in this kit.
- Scoring of the partner's logs with the open source command in the repository, run locally by the partner.
- A record of what held and what failed, reported as found, including failures.

## What is not offered or claimed

No certification, compliance statement or approval. No funding, purchase or paid standard is assumed. No data leaves the partner unless the partner chooses to share scoring results. No GNSS signal of any kind is generated.

## Next step

A short conversation to agree the operating domain, the robot, and a preregistration of thresholds before the first run.
